### Page Overview
This page is the login page for the Saveetha Engineering College Workspace. It is designed to allow students to securely access their academic portal and institutional services. The page is divided into two main sections: the left sidebar and the right login form.

### 1. Actions and Transactional Parameters
The page controls the following actions and transactional parameters:
- **Sign-in**: Users can sign in using their credentials.
- **Access**: Users can access their class schedules, campus events, seminars, workshops, request letters, official documents, and academic progress.
- **24/7 Access**: Users can access the system 24/7 from anywhere.
- **New User**: Users can request access if they are new to the system.

### Structural Explanation
The page is designed to be user-friendly and informative. The left sidebar provides a quick overview of the services available, while the right login form is the primary entry point for users to access the system. The layout ensures that users can easily find the information they need and complete the sign-in process. The additional links and help text provide additional support and guidance for new users.
